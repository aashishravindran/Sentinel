# 2026 Product & Engineering Roadmap

**STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL — Finance & Engineering Only**

This document contains forward-looking plans that are not public. Do not share outside of Finance and Engineering leadership.

## Strategic Themes

1. **AI-native workflows** — embed LLM-powered steps natively into the workflow builder
2. **Enterprise hardening** — SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SCIM, audit logs, data residency controls
3. **EMEA expansion** — product localisation + regional data residency for EU customers
