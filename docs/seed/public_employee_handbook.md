# Employee Handbook

## Working Hours & Flexibility

Acme Corp operates on a flexible-first model. Core collaboration hours are 10am–3pm in your local timezone. Outside of those hours, employees are trusted to manage their own schedules.

## Time Off

- **PTO:** 20 days per year, accrued monthly. Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days.
- **Sick Leave:** Unlimited. No approval needed for absences under 3 consecutive days.
- **Public Holidays:** Employees follow the public holidays of their country of residence.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers, 8 weeks for secondary caregivers.

## Benefits

- Health, dental, and vision insurance (US employees: premiums 100% covered for employee, 80% for dependents)
- $2,000/year learning & development budget
- $500/year home office stipend
- 401(k) with 4% company match (US); pension contributions per local law (international)

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect, operate with transparency, and escalate conflicts through their manager or People team. Discrimination and harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

## Communication Tools

- **Slack** — async messaging and team channels
- **Linear** — project and issue tracking
- **Notion** — documentation and knowledge base
- **Zoom** — video calls

## Performance Reviews

Reviews are conducted bi-annually (January and July). Compensation adjustments are tied to the January cycle. Promotions can happen at either cycle.
