# Company Overview

## About Acme Corp

Acme Corp is a B2B SaaS company founded in 2018, headquartered in San Francisco, CA. We build workflow automation tools for mid-market enterprises across manufacturing, logistics, and financial services.

## Mission

To eliminate manual, repetitive work so that people can focus on decisions that matter.

## Products

- **Acme Flow** — drag-and-drop workflow builder for non-technical teams
- **Acme Connect** — integration hub supporting 200+ third-party connectors
- **Acme Insights** — reporting and analytics layer on top of workflow data

## Company Size

~350 employees across engineering, sales, customer success, and operations. Offices in San Francisco, New York, and London.

## Key Milestones

- 2018: Founded, seed round of $3M
- 2019: Series A, $12M. First enterprise customer.
- 2021: Series B, $45M. Launched Acme Connect.
- 2023: Reached 500 enterprise customers.
- 2025: Series C, $120M. International expansion.
